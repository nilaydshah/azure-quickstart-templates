7. Root Cause Summary

Root CauseOps AffectedAvg E2EComponentSeverity
SRP slow PremiumV2_LRS provisioning (async 202) 7 of 20 ~183s SRP Critical
SRP exclusive lock contention (timeout + 409) 5 of 20 ~98s SRP High
ARM 429 subscription throttling (60s backoff) 9 of 20 ~69s ARM High
Contributing Factor: All 5,000 AllocateDisks operations occurred in a burst during 16:14–16:25 UTC, creating a thundering herd that overwhelmed both ARM's per-subscription rate limits and SRP's PremiumV2_LRS provisioning capacity in eastus2euap.

8. Recommendations

Short-term

  • ARM Throttling — Backoff Strategy: Replace the fixed 60-second backoff after 429 with exponential backoff with jitter to reduce head-of-line blocking and spread retry load.
  • ARM Rate Limits: Request a higher ARM rate limit for internal DiskRP-to-ARM calls (currently hitting SubscriptionRequestsThrottled).
  • SRP Lock Contention: Engage SRP team to investigate why PremiumV2_LRS storage account creation holds exclusive locks for 18+ seconds, causing cascading 409 failures under concurrent load.

Medium-term

  • SRP Async Provisioning: Investigate with SRP team why PremiumV2_LRS accounts take 3–4 minutes to provision asynchronously in eastus2euap. Compare with other regions and SKUs.
  • Client-side Rate Limiting: Implement batching or rate limiting in DiskManager to avoid overwhelming ARM and SRP during large-scale VM deployments.
  • Pre-provisioned Storage Accounts: Evaluate expanding the pre-provisioned storage account pool for PremiumV2_LRS to avoid on-demand SRP calls during allocation.

Long-term

  • Pipeline Parallelism: Review whether ArmRegistrationBlock and StorageAccountAllocatorBlock can run in parallel instead of sequentially to reduce E2E latency.
  • Monitoring & Alerting: Add alerts for SRP CreateStorageAccountAsync latency > 30s and ARM 429 rates exceeding thresholds.
