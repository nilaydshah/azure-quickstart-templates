🚦 Throttle Analysis

📝 Findings & Recommendations

✅ NIC Throttling Eliminated
Zero NIC 429s. CRP's internal NrpCallback creates NICs at 10ms P50 without hitting ARM rate limits. This was the #1 bottleneck in all prior runs.
✅ Massive Speed Improvement
25 min vs 63 min (Run 5). 3,440 VMs created in first 2 minutes. All ARM PUTs completed within 6 minutes; remaining time is async provisioning.
⚠️ Disk Throttling Increased
19,132 Disk 429s (vs 1,645 in Run 5). The burst of 15,000 disk PUTs in <3 min overwhelms ARM rate limits. Consider staggering or using managed disk pools.
⚠️ Zone 1 Capacity Limits
14 VMs failed in Zone 1 due to OverconstrainedZonalAllocationRequest. Allocate more VMs to Zone 3 or add Zone 1 fallback logic.
💡 Batch Size Opportunity
With 1 ARM resource per VM, the ARM limit of 800 resources/deployment means batch sizes up to 800 are possible. Current: 500. Test with 800 for fewer concurrent deployments.
💡 CRP VM PUT Latency
P50=793ms, P90=3.6s, P99=10.8s. The P90-P99 spike suggests queuing at scale. The first 2 minutes show lowest latency; later batches see contention.
