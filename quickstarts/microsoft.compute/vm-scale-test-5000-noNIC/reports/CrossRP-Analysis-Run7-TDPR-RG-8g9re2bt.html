9. Findings & Recommendations

✅ What Worked

Inline NIC Config: Eliminated 24K+ NIC 429s (was #1 bottleneck). CRP's NrpCallback is 100× faster than ARM NIC PUTs.
Larger Batches: 500 VMs/batch (vs 250) with 1 resource/VM means fewer deployments and less ARM overhead.
DiskRP Performance: 14,946 disks at 2ms avg, 100% success. DiskRP internal pipeline is not a bottleneck.

⚠️ Opportunities

Stagger Disk PUTs: 15K disk PUTs in 2 min → 19K 429s. Adding 30s delay between batch starts could reduce throttling.
Reduce GET Polling: 859K VM GETs is excessive. Increase polling interval from 5s to 15s to reduce 97K GET 429s.
Zone Allocation: Route more VMs to Zone 3 (100% success) vs Zone 1 (0.6% failure rate).
Batch Size 800: With 1 resource/VM, ARM allows up to 800 resources per deployment. Testing batch=800 would reduce from 10 to 7 deployments.
